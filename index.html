What it is

Secqr Photo Transfer is a desktop app that moves photos, videos, and text between your phone and computer over your local network.

You scan a QR code on your phone, pick or capture what you need, and it appears on your PC—or send files back to your phone. No cloud uploads, no extra apps to re-download from, no manual saving from chat or Drive.

Why Secqr

  • No cloud uploads — Files go device-to-device; no Google Drive, Dropbox, or email.
  • No chat workarounds — No need to send to WhatsApp, Discord, or Telegram and re-download.
  • Straight to a folder — Received photos and videos save directly into a folder you choose on your computer.
  • Text too — Copy text or links on one device and deliver them to the other without emailing yourself.
  • Your network only — Transfer over your Wi‑Fi; you stay in control of your files.

Features

Built for privacy first

Secqr is designed so your photos, videos, and snippets of text stay between your devices. Transfers move over an encrypted connection on your own network; they are not stored in long‑term buckets, sold to advertisers, or reused for big‑corp analytics or training.

Local network by default

Transfers happen over your Wi‑Fi or local network whenever possible. You stay in control of which devices can connect, and you can see when a new browser or machine joins a session.

Session‑based security

Each transfer session has its own ID and QR code. Expired or invalid sessions are rejected, and you can close a session at any time so that QR codes and links stop working.

End‑to‑end control

You decide which devices can join a session and when it ends. Close the desktop app or stop the session and new scans stop working—no lingering links or public inbox to clean up later.

Clear audit of where files go

You choose the destination folder on your computer (with Secqr’s default folder as a safe starting point). You always know where files land and can keep them inside encrypted or backed‑up directories you already trust.

Multi‑device friendly

Use Secqr across desktops and laptops; your phone connects with whichever machine you have open. Keep work and personal devices separated while still moving files between them in seconds.

FAQ

What is Secqr Photo Transfer?
It’s a desktop app (plus a web app on your phone) that moves photos, videos, and text between your phone and computer. You connect by scanning a QR code (or soon, opening a link), then send what you need either way—no need to upload to Google Drive, WhatsApp, or Discord and re-download.
Do I need to install something on my phone?
No native app. You open a link in your browser (the same link encoded in the QR). You can add that page to your home screen so it behaves like an app.
Where do my files go on my computer?
Photos and videos are saved in a folder you choose. By default it’s a folder like “Secqr” in your Pictures directory; you can change this in the desktop app settings or point it at an encrypted or synced folder you already use.
Can I send from computer to phone too?
Yes. You pick “PC → Phone,” then scan the QR (or open the link) on your phone. Files you select on the desktop—photos, short clips, or text—are sent to your phone, where you can save to Photos/Gallery, download, or paste.
What are “temporary” QR codes?
Premium feature. You set an expiry (e.g. 10 or 60 minutes). After that, that QR/link stops working. Good for one-time shares or when you don’t want a long-lived session.
Will there be a way to transfer without scanning the QR?
Yes. We’re adding shareable links for each session. You’ll be able to open the same session in your phone browser via a URL, so you can transfer without scanning—e.g. from another device or when you can’t use the camera. Currently in development.
Is there a limit on how many files I can transfer?
Free accounts have a limit (e.g. 5 transfers per direction per day). Premium has unlimited transfers. “Transfer” here means one send operation (which can include multiple files in that batch).
Are my photos uploaded to your servers?
Transfers are relayed only as needed to connect your phone and computer and are not stored long term. We don’t keep copies on our servers, don’t sell your data, and don’t share it with third‑party ad networks.
What do I need to run the desktop app?
Windows, macOS, or Linux. Download the installer from the website (or GitHub Releases), install, then sign in and follow the in-app steps.
How do I get Premium?
In the app, open Profile and use “Upgrade to Premium.” You’ll complete payment in your browser; after that, Premium features (unlimited transfers, temporary QR codes) are available. If the app still shows Free, use “Refresh plan status” or restart the app.
