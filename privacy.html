Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-02-06

This Privacy Policy explains how Secqr Photo Transfer ("Secqr", "we", "us") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use our website, desktop application, and companion phone web app (collectively, the "Services").

Important: This document is a general privacy policy template adapted to Secqr’s features. It is not legal advice. If you operate in a specific jurisdiction (e.g., EU/UK/California) or have unique data flows, have counsel review it for your business.

Summary

  • What Secqr does: helps you transfer photos, videos, and text between your phone and computer.
  • No “data selling” business model: we do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third-party ad networks for targeted advertising.
  • No long-term storage of transfer content: transferred files and text are relayed only as needed to provide the Services and are not stored long term by us.
  • Account/login data: if you sign in, we process basic account identifiers to operate your account and secure sessions.

Information we collect

A. Information you provide

  • Account information: when you sign in, we may receive an identifier (e.g., email address, name, profile photo) from your identity provider (such as Google), depending on what you authorize.
  • Support communications: if you contact support, we collect the information you include (such as email address and message content).

B. Transfer content (photos, videos, text)

The Services may handle content you choose to transfer, including photos, videos, and text. We do not store this content long term. Content is relayed only as needed to complete a transfer or maintain an active session, then discarded.

C. Automatically collected information

  • Device and usage data: IP address, device type, browser type, OS version, app version, timestamps, and basic event logs (e.g., session created, session closed) for security, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention.
  • Identifiers for sessions: session IDs and tokens used to connect your phone and computer.

How we use information

  • Provide the Services: establish sessions, relay transfers, and deliver files/text to the destination device.
  • Security and fraud prevention: detect abuse, enforce rate limits, and protect accounts and sessions.
  • Support and communication: respond to your requests.
  • Compliance: comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

Legal bases (EEA/UK)

If you are in the EEA/UK, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Contract: to provide the Services you request.
  • Legitimate interests: to secure the Services, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.
  • Consent: where required for certain optional features or communications.

How we share information

We share information only as needed to operate Secqr:

  • Service providers (processors): hosting/infrastructure, authentication, payment processing (if you purchase Premium), and customer support tools. They are authorized to process data only to provide services to us.
  • Legal and safety: if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and integrity of the Services.
  • Business transfers: if we undergo a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, information may be transferred as part of that transaction (subject to this Policy).

“Sale” / “Share” (California)

Secqr does not sell personal information, and does not share personal information with third parties for cross‑context behavioral advertising.

Data retention

  • Transfer content: not stored long term. Relayed only as needed to complete a transfer/session, then discarded.
  • Account and security logs: retained only as long as necessary for security, compliance, and support, then deleted or anonymized.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information. No system is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights and choices

  • Access / deletion / correction: you may request access to, deletion of, or correction of certain personal information.
  • Opt-out of sale/share: not applicable because we do not sell/share for targeted ads.
  • Cookies: this site does not require cookies to function; if we add analytics later, we will update this policy and, where required, request consent.

Children’s privacy

The Services are not intended for children under 13 (or the age required by your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

International transfers

If we transfer personal information across borders, we take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place (such as contractual protections).

Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and update the effective date above.
